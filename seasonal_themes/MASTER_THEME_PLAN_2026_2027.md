## Standard Theme Package Required Before Approval
Every seasonal theme should be completed as a review package before public release:
1. Desktop homepage mockup
2. Mobile homepage mockup
3. Hero/banner artwork
4. Companion background treatment
5. Seasonal companion outfit concepts
6. Store collection concept and pricing review
7. Seasonal promotional copy
8. Email/social graphics
9. Optional ambience/music concept
10. Start/end dates
11. Final QA checklist
12. Explicit YES/NO launch approval

## Approval Workflow
- BUILD/DESIGN: prepare full theme package.
- PREVIEW: show the completed theme to Simone before launch.
- EVALUATE: revise anything he wants changed.
- APPROVE: launch only after explicit approval for that specific seasonal theme.
- ARCHIVE: keep the approved version saved so it can be reused or evolved later.

## Core Brand Rules Across Every Theme
- What Bout Us™ branding remains clearly visible.
- Do not replace the core brand identity with holiday graphics.
- Keep navigation, companion identities, pricing logic, sign-in/account structure, and support identity consistent unless separately approved.
- Seasonal styling should feel premium and cinematic, not cheap or cartoonish unless specifically requested.
- No Nike, Adidas, Under Armour, or other name-brand logos/marks unless explicitly approved later.
- Holiday themes are overlays/evolutions of What Bout Us™, not separate websites.
